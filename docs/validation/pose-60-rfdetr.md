# RF-DETR Keypoint Preview — 60-frame diagnostic comparison

25 November 2026. This run applies the official exported RF-DETR Keypoint Preview ONNX model to the same 60 fixed 640x360 BGR frames used by [pose-60-baseline.json](pose-60-baseline.json). The baseline is the current FoodVision YOLO11x-pose plus YOLO26l-detect sequence. RF-DETR preprocessing follows export metadata: BGR to RGB, 576x576 bilinear resize, and ImageNet normalization. Decoding selects only exported class `1` (`person`) and applies the upstream top-k selection and keypoint trace-confidence fusion.

The GPU probe warmed up once, confirmed `CUDAExecutionProvider`, and then ran the frames serially. The per-frame median ONNX inference time was **16.32 ms**; directly recorded model-plus-decoder median was **19.47 ms**; full RF-DETR probe median including preprocessing was **25.34 ms**. The baseline two-model median is **34.33 ms**, but that baseline excludes preprocessing. Thus the compatible timing comparison is baseline 34.33 ms versus RF-DETR model-plus-decoder 19.47 ms. These are independently recorded per-frame metrics, not calculations from separate medians. This single-worker timing does not establish multi-camera throughput.

At raw threshold 0.08, RF-DETR emitted 0 candidates for the three title-only frames and 94–100 candidates for each other frame. For the required *diagnostic-only* threshold of 0.40, the baseline has 501 people and RF-DETR has 445. Greedy one-to-one matching at IoU >= 0.5 finds 366 overlaps: 73.1% of baseline detections and 82.2% of RF-DETR detections. Among those overlap pairs, the derived `Seated` result differs 106 times (29.0%); 235 matched pairs have a hip reported by both implementations.

This is not an accuracy or recall measurement: the baseline is not human ground truth, the 0.40 threshold was not calibrated against labels, and IoU correspondence does not validate keypoints or posture. The result supports RF-DETR as a faster one-call candidate to evaluate further, but does not support replacing the current models without labelled detection, keypoint, and seating-transition validation plus a multi-stream benchmark.
